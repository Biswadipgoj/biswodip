# Workspace Memory & Rules: biswadip.in

## Visual Design & Aesthetics Directive
1. **NO DARK BLACK AND NO PURE WHITE BACKGROUNDS**:
   - The background must NEVER be black, dark obsidian, or dim charcoal (`#000000`, `#080a0f`, `#111827`, `#0d1117`).
   - The background must NEVER be flat pure white (`#ffffff`).
   - The background MUST ALWAYS be a **vibrant, luminous Multicolor Gradient Canvas** (royal indigo, electric azure, amethyst, violet, radiant cyan, warm coral glows).

   - Crisp refractive glass borders (`border border-white/[0.22]`).
   - Spatial 3D depth, specular highlights, and ambient colorful underglows.
